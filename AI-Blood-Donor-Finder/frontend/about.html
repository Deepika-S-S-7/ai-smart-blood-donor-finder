About AI-Based Smart Blood Donor Finder

An academic technological solution engineered to address critical donor search delays during trauma cases, surgical operations, and severe blood shortages.

The Critical Challenge in Emergency Blood Acquisition

During medical emergencies, finding compatible blood is often delayed by fragmented communication, outdated static donor contact lists, and lack of real-time availability tracking. Traditional donor lists return random contacts without evaluating whether the donor is currently available, nearby, or medically eligible based on mandatory rest intervals.

The Smart Blood Donor Finder replaces static directories with an intelligent rule-based matching engine that dynamically prioritizes donors most likely to deliver blood rapidly and safely to the patient's hospital bed.

🧠

The 100-Point Rule-Based Matching Algorithm

In accordance with project design, the AI component does not use opaque machine learning black boxes. Instead, it utilizes an interpretable, mathematically transparent multi-factor weighted ranking algorithm:

1. Blood Compatibility 50 Points Max

Strict Red Blood Cell (RBC) antigen check. Identical blood group grants 50 pts; alternative compatible donor (e.g., O- for A+) grants 45 pts. Incompatible donors receive 0 points and are strictly excluded from results.

2. Location Proximity 25 Points Max

Calculated based on hospital city and neighborhood. Same city & same neighborhood: 25 pts. Same city, different neighborhood: 15 pts. Different city: 5 pts.

3. Live Availability 15 Points Max

Real-time donor toggle. Donors with status "Available" receive 15 pts. "Temporarily Unavailable" donors receive 0 pts and are pushed to the bottom of the rankings.

4. Safe Donation Recency 10 Points Max

Medical safety interval: >= 90 days since last donation (or first-time donor) grants 10 pts. Donors within 60-89 days receive 5 pts. Donors with < 60 days rest period receive 0 pts.

Total Score Formula: Score = Compat(50) + Location(25) + Availability(15) + Recency(10)
Displayed as "Application Match Score: XX%" with clear ranking tiers: Optimal Match (>= 85%), High Match (70-84%), Moderate Match (50-69%).

⚠️ Academic & Medical Disclaimer

The AI-Based Smart Blood Donor Finder is an academic engineering project developed for university coursework. It functions exclusively as a communication and search prioritization aid. It does not provide medical diagnoses, clinical advice, or replace standard blood bank procedures such as blood grouping, antibody screening, and laboratory cross-matching. In life-threatening emergencies, always contact emergency medical services (108 / 112 / 104) and certified blood banks immediately.
