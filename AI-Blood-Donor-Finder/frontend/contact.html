Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Who is eligible to register as a donor?

Any healthy individual between 18 and 65 years of age weighing at least 45 kg, with no recent blood donations in the last 90 days, can register.

How does the rule-based AI algorithm rank donors?

The engine assigns up to 50 points for blood compatibility, 25 for location proximity, 15 for live availability, and 10 for safe donation recency (total 100 points).

Can an incompatible donor ever be matched?

Never. Strict medical Red Blood Cell compatibility filters are applied first. Incompatible blood types receive 0 points and are strictly eliminated from search results.

Can donors temporarily pause incoming requests?

Yes! Donors can toggle their status switch to "Temporarily Unavailable" on their dashboard at any time to avoid notifications when sick, traveling, or resting.
